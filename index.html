EXICOM

My Exicom crew

The people I worked and learned with during my EV battery testing internship at Exicom.

Exicom crew · EV Battery Testing Intern, Sep 2025 – May 2026

Nine months at Exicom taught me more than a classroom could. From September 2025 to May 2026, I worked alongside this team on EV battery testing and analysis, including Battery Management System (BMS) testing, capacity testing on Chroma equipment, and charge-discharge testing with software tools.

Day by day I observed how testing procedures and quality control were followed, learned about battery systems and hardware components, and took on the tasks assigned to me as part of the team. It showed me what it means to work where every reading has to be right and every result has to be explained.

I am grateful to everyone in this photo for their guidance and the good moments we shared. The habits I picked up here, careful measurement, honest reporting and teamwork, are the ones I want to carry into my AI and ML career.
